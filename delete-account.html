Delete Your Account

Developer: Show Team · App: Show Team (showteam.app)

You can permanently delete your Show Team account and the data associated with it at any time — directly inside the app, or by sending us a request. This page explains both, and exactly what is removed.

Deleting your account is permanent and cannot be undone. If you'd like to keep a copy of your records first, open the app and use More → Export full backup before you delete.

Option 1 — Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Show Team and sign in.
  2. Go to the More tab.
  3. Tap Delete account (near Sign out).
  4. Type DELETE to confirm, then tap Delete my account.

Your account and data are removed immediately when you confirm.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you can't sign in, email us from the address on your account and we'll delete it for you:

Email support@showteam.app

Please send it from your account's email address (or tell us that address) so we can verify the request. We'll confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days, and usually much sooner.

What gets deleted

When you delete your account, we permanently remove:

  • Your sign-in and email address (your authentication account).
  • Your name and role within any team.
  • Your push-notification subscription, if you enabled notifications.

If you are the team owner

Deleting your account deletes the entire team you own and all of its records — animals, weigh-ins, feed programs, tasks, evaluations, shows, health and expense logs, notes, and all photos and videos — for every member of that team. This cannot be undone.

If you are a team member (not the owner)

Deleting your account removes you and your access from the team. The team's shared records stay with the remaining members, because they belong to the team the owner controls.

What may be kept, and for how long

DataRetention after deletion
Account, team records, media (per the rules above)Deleted immediately on confirmation; residual copies in encrypted backups are purged on our providers' normal backup cycle (within 30 days).
Transactional email logs (e.g. an invitation that was sent)Retained briefly by our email provider for deliverability and abuse prevention, then expire automatically.
Records we're legally required to keepOnly where required by law; deleted when no longer required.

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising. For the full picture, see our Privacy Policy.
