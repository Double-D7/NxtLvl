## Description — 4000 char max

The daily operating system for serious show-livestock families.

Show Team turns the chaos of a show season into a plan you can run every morning — one animal, one barn, one team. Whether you're feeding one county-fair barrow or a full string of pigs, lambs, goats, and cattle, Show Team keeps everyone on pace for the ring.

KNOW WHAT TODAY NEEDS
Open the app and the Barn Pulse tells you who's on plan, who needs attention, what's due to be weighed, and what tasks are still open. No guessing.

A GAME PLAN FOR EVERY ANIMAL
Set a target weight and show date, and Show Team does the math — required daily gain, projected show weight, and whether each animal is ahead, on plan, or falling behind. It's an honest read, not a guess.

DO CHORES ONE-HANDED
Barn Mode shows each animal's feed and today's tasks in a big, glove-friendly checklist. Mark it fed, check it off, move to the next pen — phone in one hand.

NEVER RUN OUT OF FEED
Log what you buy in bulk and Show Team tracks cost-per-pound automatically, estimates daily use from your active rations, and tells you how many days of each feed you have left — with a shopping list ready to go.

TRACK THE COACH'S EYE
Score muscle, shape, finish, and freshness over time and watch each animal improve. Scores are a human coach's read — the app never auto-grades your livestock.

WALK INTO EVERY SHOW READY
Show readiness at a glance: who's on weight, who still needs weighed, withdrawal-time warnings, a packing list, and show-day mode.

BUILT FOR A TEAM
Invite family, advisors, and helpers. Assign animals, leave feedback, and keep everyone in sync — even the kid doing chores has exactly what they need.

RECORD BOOKS, HANDLED
Hours, expenses, journal entries, and results captured as you go — so record book season isn't a scramble.

WORKS IN THE BARN
Fast, dark, and built mobile-first for real barn conditions. Your data syncs across every device on your team.

Pigs. Lambs. Goats. Cattle. One team. Show Team.

Questions or feedback? We're a show family too — reach us at showteam.app.
