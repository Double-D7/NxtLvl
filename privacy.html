Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Show Team is a tool for show-livestock families to manage their animals. We built it to keep your records and your family's information private and under your control. This policy explains what we collect, why, who helps us run the service, and the choices you have.

Short version: we collect the account details and the livestock records you enter so the app can work and sync across your team's devices. We don't sell your data, we don't show ads, and we don't use third-party advertising or tracking. You can export or permanently delete everything at any time.

Who we are

This service is operated by Show Team ("Show Team", "we", "us"). You can reach us about anything in this policy at support@showteam.app.

Information we collect

Account information

When you create an account we collect your email address and a password (your password is handled by our authentication provider and is never visible to us). You may also provide your name and role within a team.

The records you enter

Show Team stores the information you choose to put into it, which may include: animals and their identifiers, weigh-ins, feed programs and supplements, tasks, hair & hide and scorecard evaluations, shows and results, health and medication logs, expenses and income, notes, project/record-book details, and photos and videos you upload.

Team information

If you invite family members, advisors, or helpers, we store the email addresses and roles you assign so they can access the animals you share with them. People you invite become part of your team's shared records.

Notifications (optional)

If you turn on push notifications, we store the technical subscription details your browser or device provides (and your time-zone offset, so we can respect your quiet hours). You can turn this off at any time.

Technical information

Like any online service, our infrastructure providers process standard technical data needed to deliver the app securely and reliably (for example, network requests and error logs). We do not use third-party advertising or analytics trackers.

How we use information

  • To provide the app: store your records and sync them across your team's devices.
  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To send account- and team-related email, such as invitations you choose to send and password resets.
  • To send the notifications you opt into.
  • To operate, maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the service.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

How your data is protected

  • All traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Database access is protected by row-level security, so a team's data is isolated to that team.
  • Passwords are managed by our authentication provider and are never stored or seen by us in readable form.

No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

Your choices and rights

  • Access & correct: you can view and edit your records in the app at any time.
  • Export: you can export a full backup of your data from More → Export full backup.
  • Delete: you can permanently delete your account and its data from More → Delete account (also available on request at support@showteam.app). Deleting an account you own removes that team's records, media, and memberships.
  • Notifications: turn push on or off in the app's notification settings.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights over your personal information (such as access, correction, deletion, or portability). Contact us and we'll help you exercise them.

Children's privacy

Show Team supports youth livestock programs such as 4-H and FFA, but accounts are intended to be created and managed by parents, guardians, or adult leaders. The app is not directed to children under 13 for independent sign-up, and we do not knowingly collect personal information directly from a child under 13 without the involvement of a parent, guardian, or responsible adult who controls the account. If you believe a child provided us information without that involvement, contact us and we'll remove it.

Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service. When you delete your account, we delete the associated records and media, except where we're required to retain certain information to comply with legal obligations.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we'll change the "Last updated" date above, and significant changes will be reflected here. Your continued use of Show Team after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact us

Questions, requests, or concerns about your privacy? Email support@showteam.app.
