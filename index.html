Run your entire show barn from one place

Show Team is the daily operating system for serious show-livestock families — weights & average daily gain, feed programs, medications & withdrawal, game plans, shows and record books. It tells your whole barn what needs to happen today and whether every animal is on track for show day.

Everything your barn needs, in your pocket

Plan → Do → Track → Adjust → Show. Not a record-keeping app — a command center for the whole operation.

⚖️

Weights & ADG

One-tap weigh-ins with automatic average daily gain, projected show weight, and the gain you still need to hit target — with anomaly checks so a bad weigh-in can't skew the story.
🌾

Feed & Feed Room

Versioned feed programs that never erase history, plus a barn feed room that estimates daily use, days of supply, and builds your shopping list before you run out.
🎯

Game Plan

Set a target weight and show date and Show Team coaches every animal to it — required daily gain, projected finish, and an on-plan / behind / heavy read at a glance.
🏆

Show Mode

Each show is a hub: readiness snapshot, a reusable packing list, and feed-to-pack math that figures exactly how much feed to load from each animal's ration.
📗

Record Books

A career achievement record plus a readiness score that tells a 4-H or FFA family exactly what's still missing before the book is due — printable / save-as-PDF.
👥

Team & coaches

Owners, exhibitors, coaches and helpers — with animal-level access, a recommend → review → accept flow, and an auto-generated weekly brief for every helper.

Species-specific IDs and workflows for the whole trailer.

Why families run on Show Team

Works in the barn

Offline-capable and mobile-first — big touch targets and a daylight Barn Mode for bright, gloves-on chores with spotty signal.

💵

True cost of gain

Buy feed in bulk once; cost flows into every animal automatically from the rations they eat, so you always know cost per pound of gain and net.
💊

Show-safe meds

Log a medication and Show Team tracks its withdrawal window, warning you when a show falls inside it. It never suggests doses — you enter those from the label or your vet.
